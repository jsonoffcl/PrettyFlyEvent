Weddings

Ceremony and reception, built and dressed on schedule.

What the day looks like

We walk the venue ahead of time, plan the build around your timeline, and arrive early enough that the room is finished before anyone needs to see it. Between ceremony and reception we flip the space while guests are elsewhere.

At the end of the night we strike everything and hand the venue back clean, so nobody in your family is stacking chairs at midnight.

  • Ceremony styling, aisle, and arch
  • Reception layout and table settings
  • Florals and large installations
  • Backdrops and draping
  • Lighting and candle work
  • Signage, seating charts, stationery
  • Ceremony-to-reception flip
  • Load-in, build, and end-of-night strike
